--- layout: lecture-home title: Network Performance Analysis ---

Archive of Network Performance Analysis, taught at the University of Sheffield from 2001 to 2006 (COM 6131 with COM 3270, later COM 4290). The material covers probability review, Poisson processes, birth–death chains, Markov queues, Little's formula, Erlang delay and loss, $M/G/1$, networks of queues, and ALOHA. The 2006–07 rooms, calendar, and reading list are under Worksheets as Course information.

Sources were originally LyX notes and FoilTeX slides. They are being converted to LaMD. The 2005–2006 student website snapshot remains under published/.
