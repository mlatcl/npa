\section{How the papers are presented}

\notes{The Sheffield papers were two-hour unseen exams, with a masters variant (COM6131) and an undergraduate variant (COM3270) sharing most questions. The compiled sheets here are *questions only*. Instructor answers, mark allocations in italics, and the 2005 worked script `examAnswers2005.ps` remain in the repository and are not compiled to the site. That matches how the 2006 class saw the paper, and it keeps the archive usable as practice without publishing the scheme.}

\notes{Each sitting is its own worksheet: *Exam 2002--03*, *Exam 2004--05*, *Exam 2005--06*. Historic PostScript (`exam2004.ps`, `exam2005.ps`) stays in the repo root. The draft file `exam.tex` is incomplete and is not converted. Erlang B/C curves issued with the paper are the same rasters as in the Erlang lectures.}

\section{What each paper examines}

\notes{*2002--03.* Four questions, no A/B split. Q1 is residual service time and Pollaczek--Khinchin ($M/G/1$, lectures 11--13). Q2 is an ns-2 multipath listing and Kleinrock (Lab 2 and lectures 14--15). Q3 is Erlang C on a call-centre merger (lectures 9 and 6). Q4 is the finite $M/M/m/n$ birth-death diagram (lectures 5, 8--10).}

\notes{*2004--05.* Section A is one compulsory birth-death survey (utilisation, blocking, existence of steady state: lectures 5--10). Section B offers a choice: the discrete $M/M/1$ simulator from Lab 1 (Q2), an ARQ delay as an $M/G/1$ service time (Q3; lectures 11--13), or Kleinrock and Jackson on tandem queues (Q4; lectures 14--15).}

\notes{*2005--06.* Section A is Pollaczek--Khinchin (Q1). Section B is Erlang B (Q2; lecture 10), an Erlang C call-centre merger with issued curves (Q3; lecture 9), or ns-2 plus Kleinrock and a warning about sample averages (Q4; Lab 1, Lab 2, lecture 14).}

\notes{The papers are the practice set for the whole course, including $M/G/1$, Kleinrock/Jackson, and the labs.}
