\section{Rooms}

\notes{From the 2006--07 notes. Mondays 15:10--17:00 (St Georges LT17). Tuesdays 16:10--17:00 (St Georges LT2). Lab classes in the Lewin Lab on the Mondays of weeks 3 and 8 (the notes also mention a week-9 lab review of ns-2).}

\section{Calendar}

\notes{Monday is a two-hour slot; Tuesday is one hour. Lecture numbers are those used in the notes.}

\notes{*Week 1.* Monday lectures 1--2, networks and probability review. Tuesday lecture 3, Poisson processes.}

\notes{*Week 2.* Monday lectures 4--5, birth-death processes and $M/M/1$. Tuesday lecture 6, Little's formula.}

\notes{*Week 3.* Monday lab class 1, discrete simulation of $M/M/1$. Tuesday tutorial 1, probability and Poisson processes.}

\notes{*Week 4.* Monday lectures 7--8, other Markov queues and Erlang delay. Tuesday lab review 1.}

\notes{*Week 5.* Monday lectures 9--10, Erlang loss and Markov queues review. Tuesday tutorial 2, $M/M/1$ and Little's formula.}

\notes{*Week 6.* Monday lectures 11--12, $M/G/1$ and vacations. Tuesday lecture 13, $M/G/1$ continued (ARQ).}

\notes{*Week 7.* Monday lectures 14--15, networks of queues and Jackson's theorem. Tuesday tutorial 3, Erlang delay and loss, and $M/G/1$.}

\notes{*Week 8.* Monday lab class 2, simulation of queues in ns-2. Tuesday lecture 16, ALOHA.}

\notes{*Week 9.* Tuesday lab review 2, the ns-2 lab.}

\notes{*Week 10.* Exam practice: the 2004--05 paper.}

\notes{*Week 12.* Monday exam review of that paper.}

\section{Evaluation}

\notes{By 2006--07 the course was 100\% exam. In 2001--02 the discrete $M/M/1$ simulation (Lab 1) was 30\% coursework, due 21 November 2002, with code recovered from a DCS directory `COM6131a1y1` or `COM3270a1y1`.}

\section{Reading list}

\notes{The course is based primarily on Bertsekas and Gallager. The notes cover in extra detail areas of importance for the course and add material where the text is thin (simulation in particular).}

\addreading{@Bertsekas:data92}{Chapter 1, background and review; Chapter 3, Sections 3.1--3.5, queueing models; Chapter 4 in less detail, Sections 4.1--4.4, 4.5.2, 4.5.3}

\addreading{@Tanenbaum:computer96}{Standard introductory networks text; little coverage of queueing}

\addreading{@Chan:performance00}{Alternative text for details on queues}

\notes{Campus-only material originally sat at `http://www.dcs.shef.ac.uk/~neil/campus_only/com6131/`. The public 2005--06 snapshot is under `published/` in this repository.}
