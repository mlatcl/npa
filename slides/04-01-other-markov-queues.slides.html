Other Markov Queues

2006-10-16

Other Markov Queues

  • Next to \(M/M/1\): finite buffer, or infinitely many servers
  • Same detailed-balance recurrence, different \(\lambda_k\), \(\mu_k\)

Infinite Servers: \(M/M/\infty\)

State diagram for the \(M/M/\infty\) queue: unbounded \(k\), death rate rising linearly with occupancy.
Boundary flow for \(M/M/\infty\): \(\lambda P(k-1)=k\mu P(k)\).
  • \(\mu_k=k\mu\), \(\lambda_k=\lambda\)
  • Offered load \(a=\lambda/\mu\) (Erlangs), not utilisation
  • \(P(k)=e^{-a}a^k/k!\): Poisson occupancy

Finite Buffer: \(M/M/1/m\)

State diagram for \(M/M/1/m\): the chain stops at \(m\); there is no transition \(m\to m+1\).
Interior boundary flow is the same as \(M/M/1\): \(\lambda P(k-1)=\mu P(k)\).

Blocking Probability

  • \(M/M/1/m\): cap at \(m\) customers, lost arrivals do not return
  • Same \(\rho^k\) shape, truncated geometric \(P(0)=(1-\rho)/(1-\rho^{m+1})\)
  • Blocking \(P(m)\); steady state exists even for \(\rho\ge 1\)
