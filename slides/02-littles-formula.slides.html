Little’s Formula

2006-10-03

Time Averages

  • Time average \(\bar{N}_t=(1/t)\int_0^t N(\tau)\,d\tau\)
  • Steady state: \(\langle k\rangle=\lim_{t\to\infty}\bar{N}_t\)
  • Little applies to \(G/G/1\) in steady state

Little’s Formula

A Nightclub Queue

  • \(\bar{N}=\lambda\bar{T}\)
  • Nightclub: \(\bar{N}=20\), \(\lambda=40\,\mathrm{h}^{-1}\) \(\Rightarrow\) \(\bar{T}=1/2\) hour
  • Distribution-free; needs steady state

Average Delay in \(M/M/1\)

  • \(\bar{N}=\rho/(1-\rho)\) from last lecture
  • \(\bar{T}=1/(\mu-\lambda)\)
  • Includes transmission, not only waiting

Queueing Time and Transmission Time

  • \(T=X+W\), \(\rho=\lambda\bar{X}=1-P(0)\)
  • \(\bar{W}=\rho/(\mu-\lambda)\)
  • \(\bar{N}_Q=\rho^2/(1-\rho)\)

Graphical Argument

Arrivals \(\alpha(\tau)\) and departures \(\beta(\tau)\). The shaded area is both the integral of occupancy and the sum of sojourn times.

A Bar Queue

  • Area argument: \(\int N=\sum T_i\) \(\Rightarrow\) \(\bar{N}_t=\bar{\lambda}_t\bar{T}_t\)
  • Bar: \(\rho=0.9\), \(\bar{N}_Q=3\) \(\Rightarrow\) \(\bar{W}/\bar{X}=10/3\)
