Erlang Loss: \(M/M/m/m\)

Flow chart of the Erlang loss system: offered load \(a\) in, carried load \(a'<a\) out.
Markov chain for \(M/M/m/m\): the chain stops at \(m\); there is no queueing state.

Truncated Poisson

Boundary flow for \(k\le m\): \(\lambda P(k-1)=k\mu P(k)\).
  • \(M/M/m/m\): \(m\) servers, no waiting line, blocked arrivals lost
  • Same \(\lambda P(k-1)=k\mu P(k)\) as \(M/M/\infty\), truncated at \(m\)
  • Occupancy is truncated Poisson

Probability of Blocking

Historic Erlang B curves for offered traffic between 0 and 20.
Historic Erlang B curves for offered traffic between 0 and 100.
  • \(B(m,a)=P(m)\): Erlang B, fraction of arrivals lost
  • No queue, so \(P_Q=0\)
  • Log-space evaluation; exams used paper curves

Carried Load and Utilisation

  • \(a'=a(1-B(m,a))\), \(\rho=a'/m\)
  • Throughput \(\dot{\lambda}=\lambda(1-P(m))\)
  • Little: use carried rate, not offered \(\lambda\)
