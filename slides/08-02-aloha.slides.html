Multiaccess and ALOHA

2006-11-14

Multiaccess Channels

  • One channel, many stations
  • Statistical multiplexing beats TDM/FDM by factor \(m\)
  • No central queue \(\Rightarrow\) random access

Pure ALOHA

Pure ALOHA: a packet sent at \(t\) collides with any other packet sent in \((t-1,t+1)\).
Pure ALOHA throughput \(S=Ge^{-2G}\). The maximum is \(1/(2e)\approx 0.184\) at \(G=1/2\).
  • Vulnerable period of length 2
  • \(G=\lambda+n\mu\), \(S=Ge^{-2G}\)
  • \(\max S=1/(2e)\) at \(G=1/2\)

Slotted ALOHA

Slotted ALOHA throughput \(S=Ge^{-G}\). The maximum is \(1/e\) at \(G=1\).
  • Slotted: vulnerable period 1, \(S=Ge^{-G}\)
  • \(\max S=1/e\) at \(G=1\)
  • Backlog wait exponential; best \(\mu=1/(2n)\)
