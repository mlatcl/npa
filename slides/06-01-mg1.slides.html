The M/G/1 Queue

2006-10-30

\(M/G/1\) and Pollaczek–Khinchin

  • General service: no birth-death chain
  • Residual service time \(\bar{R}\)
  • Pollaczek–Khinchin: \(\bar{W}=\lambda\langle X^2\rangle/(2(1-\rho))\)

Assumptions

  • FIFO; arrivals independent of service; finite \(\mathrm{Var}(X)\)
  • \(\bar{W}=\bar{R}/(1-\rho)\)

Residual Time from Completions

Residual service time over a busy period: a sawtooth of triangles with sides \(X_i\). \(M(t)\) is the number of completions in \([0,t]\).
  • \(A(t)=\frac12\sum X_i^2\)
  • \(\bar{R}=\frac12\lambda\langle X^2\rangle\)
  • \(\bar{T}=\bar{X}+\bar{W}\); Little for \(\bar{N}_Q\), \(\bar{N}\)

Residual Time from an Arrival

The \(i\)th arrival sees residual \(R_i\) on the customer in service and waiting times \(X\) for each of the \(k_i-1\) customers already queued.
  • \(W_i=R_i+\) work already present
  • Independence + Little \(\Rightarrow\) \(\bar{W}=\bar{R}/(1-\rho)\)

Special Cases

  • \(M/M/1\): PK recovers \(\bar{W}=\rho/(\mu(1-\rho))\)
  • \(M/D/1\): half that wait; lowest delay at fixed mean
  • \(M/U/1\) on \([0,b]\): \(\langle X^2\rangle=4/(3\mu^2)\)

\(M/G/1\) with Vacations

An \(M/G/1\) server that takes a vacation whenever the queue is empty.
Residual time now includes vacation triangles as well as service triangles.
  • Vacation whenever idle; i.i.d. \(V_i\)
  • \(\bar{W}=\lambda\langle X^2\rangle/(2(1-\rho))+\langle V^2\rangle/(2\bar{V})\)
  • Exponential vacation adds \(\bar{V}\); deterministic adds \(\bar{V}/2\)
